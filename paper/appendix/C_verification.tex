\section{Numerical verification of the theory}\label{app:verification}

Every result of \cref{sec:invariance,sec:reparam,sec:tracking} was checked numerically in float64 on single layers or toy models (\cref{tab:verification}). These checks test the mathematics. \Cref{sec:experiments} tests real implementations.

The toy QuRL loop has INT4 rollouts, GRPO advantages, TIS, ACR, AdamW and global-norm clipping. It runs two epochs of four minibatches per rollout batch, so ACR binds 68 times and clipping fires on 20\% of the updates. Against the UAQ run:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
\item the matched run of \cref{thm:reparam} samples identical actions at every step, and its weights agree to a relative $3.2\times10^{-10}$ ($W$) and $1.3\times10^{-10}$ ($\gamma$) after 2{,}400 updates;
\item matched learning rates with ordinary clipping share only 59.7\% of the sampled actions;
\item plain settings share 50.1\%.
\end{itemize}
